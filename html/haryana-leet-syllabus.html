Important Note on Official Syllabus

The syllabus below is from the official Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES) Admission Brochure — Chapter 4: Scheme, Syllabus and Procedure for Online Entrance Test for B.E./B.Tech (LEET), issued by the Department of Higher Education / Directorate of Technical Education, Haryana. It is aligned with the Diploma Curriculum prescribed by AICTE/HSBTE, but may require exhaustive study beyond the prescribed curriculum.

Important for every student: This is a common syllabus for all diploma branches. CSE, Mechanical, Civil, ECE, Auto — everyone gets the same paper (sections a+b+c+d). Your diploma branch does not create a separate entrance syllabus.

Exam Scheme (Official)

  • Maximum Marks: 90
  • Duration: 90 minutes
  • Type: Objective MCQs in English (4 options, 1 correct)
  • Negative Marking: No
  • Common Online Entrance Test for all Engineering/Technology diploma streams seeking 2nd year (3rd semester) B.E./B.Tech lateral entry

Section a — Basic Sciences (Q 1–25 | 25 marks)

  • a-1 Mathematics: 8 questions / 8 marks
  • a-2 Physics: 8 questions / 8 marks
  • a-3 Chemistry: 3 questions / 3 marks
  • a-4 Communication Skills: 3 questions / 3 marks
  • a-5 General Awareness: 3 questions / 3 marks

Section b — Electronics Stream Courses (Q 26–50 | 25 marks)

  • b-1 Elements of Electrical Engg.: 9 / 9
  • b-2 Elements of Electronics Engg.: 8 / 8
  • b-3 Elements of Computer Engg.: 8 / 8

Section c — Mechanical Stream Courses (Q 51–70 | 20 marks)

  • c-1 Elements of Mechanical Engg.: 8 / 8
  • c-2 Elements of Production Engg. / Manufacturing Processes: 6 / 6
  • c-3 Auto Engg.: 6 / 6

Section d — Other Engineering Streams (Q 71–90 | 20 marks)

  • d-1 Civil Engg. Courses: 5 / 5
  • d-2 Textile Engg. Courses: 5 / 5
  • d-3 Chemical / Printing Technology: 2 / 2
  • d-4 Ceramic Engg.: 2 / 2
  • d-5 Food Technology: 2 / 2
  • d-6 Others: 4 / 4 (Agriculture, Architecture, Fashion Design & Technology)

(a-1) Mathematics

  • Arithmetic Progressions, Complex Numbers, Logarithm, Permutation and Combination, Binomial expansion, Probability, Matrices, Trigonometry, Straight Line, Circle
  • Differentiation of a function, Successive differentiation, Maxima and Minima
  • Definite and indefinite Integration, application of integration
  • First order and first degree ordinary differential equations
  • Mean, mode, median, mean deviation and standard deviation

(a-2) Physics

  • Units and Dimensions with Dimensional analysis and their Limitations
  • Motion in one dimension and Newton's Laws of Motion; Work and Energy and Conservation Laws of Energy
  • Properties of matter (Elasticity, surface tension, viscosity); Waves and vibration, SHM
  • Rotational motion, Conservation of angular momentum
  • Heat and temperature, modes of heat transfer and their laws
  • Geometric optics and simple optical instruments
  • Electrostatics (E and Potential), Capacitors and dielectric constant
  • Laser, superconductivity; Conventional and nonconventional energy sources

(a-3) Chemistry

  • Hard and soft water, types of hardness, disadvantages, causes and remedies
  • Acidity, Basicity, Ion Exchange Method
  • Electronic Configuration upto atomic number 30, Equivalent weight, pH value
  • Definition of symbol, formula, valency and chemical equation

(a-4) Communication Skill

  • Words, antonyms and synonyms, communication technique
  • Grammatical ability: Preposition, Correction, Voice, Narration, Punctuation, Tenses, correction of incorrect sentences

(a-5) General Awareness

  • Technical Education in Haryana — Polytechnics, sanctioned intake, LEET eligibility, institutions offering BE/B.Tech & B.Pharmacy
  • Processes involved in on-line off campus counseling
  • General awareness about Haryana (Districts/Tehsils/Blocks/villages, population, area, boundary states, history)
  • General awareness about country and Polytechnics

Section b — Electronics Stream

b-1 Electrical: Electrical & Magnetic Circuits, AC Circuits, Transformers, DC Machines, Induction Motor, Transmission and Distribution.

b-2 Electronics: Measurements & Instrumentation, Communication System, Analog Electronics, Digital Electronics, Industrial Electronics (SCR, DIAC, TRIAC).

b-3 Computer: Fundamentals of Computers, Computer Organization, Operating Systems, Programming in C, Basics of Computer Networking (LAN/WAN/Internet).

Section c — Mechanical Stream

c-1 Mechanical: Applied Mechanics, Strength of Materials, Thermodynamics, Modes of Heat Transfer, Fluid Mechanics.

c-2 Production / Manufacturing: Workshop Technology, Material Science, Engineering Graphics & Drawing, Foundry, Plastics, Industrial management.

c-3 Automobile: Power flow, Gear box, Brakes, Clutches, Differential, Cooling & lubrication, Wheels & tyres, EV/Hybrid, Steering system and angles.

Section d — Other Streams

d-1 Civil: Bricks, Soil, Water supply/treatment, Foundations, Surveying, RCC basics, Roads & bridges, Irrigation, Cement, Ecology & pollution, Non-conventional energy.

d-2 Textile: Fibers/fabrics/yarn, Weaving, Testing & QC, Bleaching/Dying/Printing.

d-3 Chemical/Printing: Fluid flow, Chemical process / agro / petro chemicals, Printing machines and types of printing.

d-4 Ceramic: Pottery products, ceramic materials & properties.

d-5 Food Technology: Vitamins, cereals & pulses, milk & milk powder, food preservation.

d-6 Others: Agriculture (farm equipment); Architecture (Indian architecture, building typologies); Fashion Design & Technology (garment machines & tools).

B.Pharmacy LEET - Broad Syllabus Areas

For B.Pharmacy lateral entry through Haryana LEET, the expected syllabus generally includes diploma-level pharmacy subjects:

  • Pharmaceutics: dosage forms, drug delivery systems, tablets, capsules, ointments, emulsions, suspensions
  • Pharmaceutical Chemistry: organic chemistry basics, medicinal chemistry fundamentals, drug classification
  • Pharmacology: general pharmacology, autonomic nervous system, cardiovascular drugs, CNS drugs
  • Pharmacognosy: crude drugs, sources, classification, active constituents
  • Pharmaceutical Analysis: titrimetric analysis, instrumentation fundamentals
  • Human Anatomy & Physiology: body systems, organs, basic functions
