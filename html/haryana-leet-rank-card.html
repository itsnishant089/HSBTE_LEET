Understanding Your Haryana LEET Rank Card

The hsbte leet rank card or hstes ocet scorecard is the single most important document for admission. It includes the following details:

  • Marks Scored: Your total score out of 90 marks in the OCET exam.
  • Inter-Se Merit (State Rank): Your overall rank among all candidates appearing in Haryana.
  • Category Rank: Your specific rank within your reserved category (e.g., BCA, BCB, SC, EWS).
  • Candidate Name, Roll Number, and Father's Name for verification.

The admissions to top government universities like YMCA, DCRUST, and GJU depend entirely on your Inter-Se Merit haryana leet rank.

Haryana LEET Inter-Se Merit Tie-Breaking Rules

If two or more candidates score the same marks in the computer based test haryana leet result, the tie is resolved using the following criteria in sequence:

  1. Candidates with higher marks in Mathematics will be ranked higher.
  2. If the tie persists, candidates with higher marks in Physics will be ranked higher.
  3. If still tied, candidates with higher marks in Chemistry will get preference.
  4. If the tie is not resolved by marks, the older candidate in age will be ranked higher.
