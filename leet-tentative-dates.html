HSBTE LEET 2026 Dates Not Released Yet

The tentative dates for HSBTE LEET 2026 examination have not been announced yet by the Haryana State Board of Technical Education (HSBTE). The official dates for registration, application submission, admit card release, examination, and counselling will be updated on this page as soon as they are released by the board.

What to Expect:

  • Application process typically starts in May-June
  • Examination usually conducted in July-August
  • Results declared within 1-2 months after examination
  • Counselling process begins after result declaration

Stay Updated: Keep checking the official HSBTE website (hsbte.org.in) and this page for the latest updates on LEET 2026 dates.
