HSBTE LEET Overview

Complete Guide to Haryana Lateral Entry Engineering Test

What is HSBTE LEET?

HSBTE LEET (Haryana State Board of Technical Education Lateral Entry Engineering Test) is a state-level entrance examination conducted for admission to second year (lateral entry) of Bachelor of Technology (BTech) and Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharmacy) courses in various engineering and pharmacy colleges across Haryana.

Key Features:

  • Conducted annually by HSBTE (Haryana State Board of Technical Education)
  • Provides lateral entry admission to 2nd year BTech/B.Pharmacy programs
  • Open for diploma holders and B.Sc graduates
  • Multiple engineering and pharmacy branches available
  • State-wide recognition and acceptance

Eligibility Criteria

For BTech LEET:

  • Educational Qualification: Candidates must have passed Diploma in Engineering/Technology from a recognized institution approved by AICTE/State Board of Technical Education, OR B.Sc degree with Mathematics as one of the subjects from a recognized university.
  • Minimum Marks: General category candidates need minimum 60% aggregate marks. Reserved category candidates (SC/ST/OBC) need minimum 55% aggregate marks.
  • Domicile Requirement: Candidates must be a resident of Haryana (as per Haryana Domicile Certificate) OR must have passed 10th and 12th standard from Haryana.
  • Age Limit: There is no upper age limit for HSBTE LEET examination.
  • Appearing Candidates: Candidates appearing in the final year/semester of Diploma/B.Sc are also eligible to apply, provided they submit their final year marksheet before the counselling process.

For B.Pharmacy LEET:

  • Educational Qualification: Candidates must have passed Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm) from a recognized institution approved by Pharmacy Council of India (PCI).
  • Minimum Marks: General category candidates need minimum 60% aggregate marks. Reserved category candidates (SC/ST/OBC) need minimum 55% aggregate marks.
  • Registration: Candidates must be registered with the State Pharmacy Council or should be eligible for registration.
  • Domicile Requirement: Candidates must be a resident of Haryana (as per Haryana Domicile Certificate) OR must have passed 10th and 12th standard from Haryana.
  • Age Limit: There is no upper age limit for B.Pharmacy LEET examination.
  • Appearing Candidates: Candidates appearing in the final year/semester of D.Pharm are also eligible to apply, provided they submit their final year marksheet and registration certificate before the counselling process.

Exam Pattern

The HSBTE LEET examination follows a standardized pattern designed to assess the candidate's knowledge and aptitude for lateral entry into engineering and pharmacy programs.

General Information:

  • Mode of Examination: Offline (Pen and Paper Based Test - PBT)
  • Duration: 2 hours (120 minutes)
  • Total Questions: 100 multiple-choice questions (MCQs)
  • Total Marks: 100 marks
  • Marking Scheme: +1 mark for each correct answer, No negative marking for incorrect answers
  • Language: Questions are in English language
  • Question Type: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) with four options each

Subject-wise Distribution (BTech LEET):

  • Mathematics: 25 questions (25 marks)
  • Physics: 25 questions (25 marks)
  • Chemistry: 25 questions (25 marks)
  • Branch-specific Engineering Subjects: 25 questions (25 marks) - Based on the branch applied for (Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, Computer Science, Electronics, etc.)

Subject-wise Distribution (B.Pharmacy LEET):

  • Pharmaceutics: 25 questions (25 marks)
  • Pharmaceutical Chemistry: 25 questions (25 marks)
  • Pharmacology: 25 questions (25 marks)
  • Pharmaceutical Analysis & Human Anatomy & Physiology: 25 questions (25 marks)

Important Instructions:

  • Candidates must bring their admit card and a valid photo ID to the examination center
  • Use only black or blue ballpoint pen to mark answers on the OMR sheet
  • No electronic devices, calculators, or study materials are allowed inside the examination hall
  • Follow all instructions given by the invigilators

Application Process

The application process for HSBTE LEET is conducted entirely online through the official HSBTE website. Follow these detailed steps to complete your application:

Step-by-Step Application Process:

  1. Visit Official Website: Go to the official HSBTE website (hsbte.org.in) or the LEET portal (techadmissionshry.gov.in)
  2. Registration: Click on "New Registration" and create an account with:
    • Valid email address (will be used for all communications)
    • Active mobile number (will receive OTP and updates)
    • Choose a strong password
    • Verify email and mobile number through OTP
  3. Login: Use your registered email and password to login to your account
  4. Fill Application Form: Complete all sections of the application form:
    • Personal Details: Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Category, Domicile details
    • Contact Information: Address, Email, Phone number
    • Academic Details: Diploma/B.Sc details, Marks, Institution name, Year of passing
    • Course Selection: Choose BTech or B.Pharmacy, select preferred branches
    • Examination Center: Select preferred examination city/center
  5. Upload Documents: Upload scanned copies of required documents (in specified format and size):
    • Recent passport-size photograph (JPG/JPEG, max 50KB)
    • Signature (JPG/JPEG, max 20KB)
    • Diploma/B.Sc marksheet and certificate
    • Domicile certificate (if applicable)
    • Category certificate (for SC/ST/OBC candidates)
    • Any other required documents
  6. Review Application: Carefully review all entered information before final submission
  7. Pay Application Fee: Pay the application fee online through:
    • Debit Card / Credit Card
    • Net Banking
    • UPI
    • Application fee: General category - ₹1000, Reserved categories - ₹500 (subject to change)
  8. Submit Application: After successful payment, submit the application form
  9. Download Confirmation: Download and print the application confirmation page for future reference
  10. Download Admit Card: Download the admit card from the website 1 week before the examination date

Important Notes:

  • Keep all original documents ready for verification during counselling
  • Application fee is non-refundable once paid
  • Corrections in application form can be made within the specified correction window
  • Keep your login credentials safe and secure
  • Check the official website regularly for updates and notifications

Important Dates

  • Application Start: Usually in May-June
  • Application End: Usually in June-July
  • Admit Card: 1 week before exam
  • Exam Date: Usually in July-August
  • Result Declaration: Within 1-2 months
  • Counselling: After result declaration

Note: Check the official website for exact dates as they may vary each year.

Preparation Tips

  • Start early and create a study schedule
  • Focus on fundamentals of Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry
  • Practice previous year question papers
  • Take mock tests regularly
  • Revise important formulas and concepts
  • Stay updated with exam notifications
  • Maintain good health and manage stress

Available Branches

HSBTE LEET offers admission to various engineering and pharmacy branches across multiple colleges in Haryana. The availability of branches may vary each year based on seat availability and college infrastructure.

BTech Branches Available:

  • Computer Science Engineering (CSE) - Most popular branch with high demand
  • Mechanical Engineering - Traditional and widely available branch
  • Civil Engineering - Infrastructure and construction focused
  • Electrical Engineering - Power systems and electronics
  • Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) - Communication systems and electronics
  • Information Technology (IT) - Software and networking
  • Automobile Engineering - Vehicle design and manufacturing
  • Chemical Engineering - Chemical processes and industries
  • Production Engineering - Manufacturing and production systems
  • Instrumentation Engineering - Control systems and instrumentation
  • Textile Engineering - Textile manufacturing and processing
  • Agricultural Engineering - Agricultural machinery and systems

B.Pharmacy:

  • Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) - 4-year undergraduate program covering pharmaceutical sciences, drug formulation, pharmacology, and pharmacy practice

Important Notes:

  • Branch availability depends on the participating colleges
  • Seat availability varies for each branch and category
  • Candidates can select multiple branches in order of preference during counselling
  • Final branch allotment depends on rank, preferences, and seat availability
  • Some branches may have limited seats, so choose wisely based on your interests and career goals

Counselling Process

The counselling process for HSBTE LEET is conducted online in multiple rounds to ensure fair and transparent seat allocation based on merit and candidate preferences.

Detailed Counselling Process:

  1. Online Registration for Counselling:
    • After result declaration, qualified candidates need to register for counselling
    • Login with your LEET application credentials
    • Fill counselling registration form
    • Pay counselling fee (if applicable)
    • Download counselling registration slip
  2. Choice Filling:
    • Browse available colleges and branches
    • Select colleges and branches in order of preference
    • You can select multiple choices (typically 50-100 choices)
    • Arrange choices in order of preference (most preferred first)
    • Save your choices (you can modify before locking)
  3. Choice Locking:
    • Review all your selected choices carefully
    • Lock your choices before the deadline
    • Once locked, choices cannot be modified
    • Download locked choices confirmation
  4. Seat Allotment:
    • Seat allotment is done based on:
      • LEET rank/merit
      • Candidate preferences (locked choices)
      • Category (General/SC/ST/OBC)
      • Seat availability in colleges
      • Reservation policies
    • Allotment results are published online
    • Download seat allotment letter
    • Check allotted college, branch, and reporting details
  5. Document Verification:
    • Report to the designated verification center on specified date
    • Carry all original documents for verification:
      • LEET Admit Card
      • LEET Rank Card
      • Seat Allotment Letter
      • Diploma/B.Sc marksheet and certificate
      • 10th and 12th standard certificates
      • Domicile certificate
      • Category certificate (if applicable)
      • Photo ID proof
      • Recent passport-size photographs
    • Get documents verified by officials
    • Collect verification slip
  6. Fee Payment and Admission Confirmation:
    • Pay admission fee and other charges online or at the college
    • Fee structure varies by college and category
    • Download fee payment receipt
    • Confirm your admission
  7. Reporting to Allotted College:
    • Report to the allotted college on the specified date
    • Submit all required documents to the college
    • Complete admission formalities
    • Collect admission letter and other documents

Counselling Rounds:

  • Round 1: First round of seat allotment for all registered candidates
  • Round 2: Second round for candidates who didn't get seat in Round 1 or want to upgrade
  • Spot Counselling: If seats remain vacant, spot counselling may be conducted

Important Points:

  • Participate in all counselling rounds to maximize your chances
  • Fill maximum choices to increase probability of getting preferred college/branch
  • Keep all documents ready and verified
  • Check counselling schedule regularly
  • If you don't report after seat allotment, you may lose the seat
  • You can participate in next round even after taking admission in previous round (upgrade option)
