How to Download Haryana LEET Admit Card 2027?

Follow these simple steps to download your admit card haryana leet 2027 for both B.Tech and B.Pharmacy courses:

  • Step 1: Click on the "Download Admit Card Now" button above to visit the official portal: techadmissionshry.gov.in.
  • Step 2: On the portal, select your respective course (e.g., BTech LEET Admit Card 2027 or BPharmacy LEET Admit Card 2027).
  • Step 3: Enter your Haryana LEET Application Number or Registration ID and your Date of Birth / Password.
  • Step 4: Click on the "Submit" or "Download" button.
  • Step 5: Your haryana leet hall ticket will appear on the screen in PDF format. Verify all details carefully.
  • Step 6: Save the PDF and take at least two color printouts to carry to the haryana leet 2027 exam center.

Details Mentioned on BTech / BPharmacy LEET Admit Card

Your hsbte leet admit card or hstes ocet admit card will contain the following critical information. Please ensure everything is printed correctly:

  • Candidate's Full Name and Photograph
  • Haryana LEET 2027 Roll Number and Application ID
  • Date of Birth and Category
  • Complete Address of the Haryana LEET Test Center
  • Haryana LEET Timings (Shift 1, Shift 2, or Shift 3)
  • Reporting Time at the Examination Center
  • Signature of the Candidate and Exam Authority
  • Important Leet Exam Day Guidelines and Instructions

If you spot any discrepancies (e.g., spelling mistake in name, wrong photo), contact the hstes helpline admit card immediately for correction.

Important Exam Day Guidelines (What to Carry)

Candidates appearing for the lateral entry engineering haryana admit card or pharmacy exams must strictly follow the rules:

  1. Printed Admit Card: A clear, preferably color printout of the downloaded techadmissionshry admit card.
  2. Valid ID Proof: Original Aadhar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Passport (leet admit card valid id proof).
  3. Photographs: Two recent passport-size photographs (same as uploaded during registration).
  4. Stationery: A transparent blue/black ballpoint pen (if required for rough work, though the test is CBT).

Note: Electronic devices, calculators, and study materials are strictly prohibited inside the btech leet online test center.
