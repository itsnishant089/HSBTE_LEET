What is HSBTE LEET?

HSBTE LEET (Haryana State Board of Technical Education Lateral Entry Engineering Test) is the common online entrance test for admission to the 2nd year (3rd semester) of Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) and Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharmacy) courses in Haryana, as per the official B.Tech (LEET) and B.Pharmacy LEET brochures.

Exam Full NameHSBTE Lateral Entry Engineering Test (LEET)
Conducting BodyHaryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES)
CoursesB.Tech (Lateral Entry), B.Pharmacy (Lateral Entry)
Admission Into2nd Year / 3rd Semester
Exam ModeCommon Online Entrance Test (Computer-Based)
Official Portaltechadmissionshry.gov.in / hstes.org.in
  • ✓Common online entrance test for all eligible diploma holders (and B.Sc. where permitted)
  • ✓Direct admission to 2nd year (3rd semester) B.Tech / B.Pharmacy in participating institutions
  • ✓Single state-level merit list used for counselling and seat allotment
  • ✓Syllabus aligned with AICTE / HSBTE diploma curriculum and pharmacy diploma syllabus

Eligibility Criteria

🔧 B.Tech LEET – Eligibility
  • ✓Qualification: 3-year Diploma in Engineering / Technology in an appropriate branch from a recognised State Board / University. Some years also permit B.Sc. (with Mathematics) — confirm in the current brochure.
  • ✓Minimum Marks: Minimum aggregate percentage as notified by HSTES for that session (category-wise). Always follow the latest prospectus instead of any fixed number mentioned elsewhere.
  • ✓Domicile & Reservation: Rules, backlog conditions and any additional requirements are exactly as given in the B.Tech LEET admission brochure.
📌Always confirm the final eligibility rules in the official HSTES B.Tech LEET brochure before applying.
💊 B.Pharmacy LEET – Eligibility
  • ✓Qualification: Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm) from an institute approved by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI).
  • ✓Minimum Marks & Registration: Category-wise minimum percentage and State Pharmacy Council registration requirements are to be followed exactly as written in the latest B.Pharmacy LEET prospectus.
📌Refer to the latest B.Pharmacy LEET prospectus on the official HSTES portal for final eligibility details.

Application Process

The application process for HSBTE LEET is conducted entirely online through the official portal. Follow these steps to complete your application:

Visit Official Website

Go to hsbte.org.in or the LEET portal techadmissionshry.gov.in and look for the LEET 2026 notification link.

New Registration

Click "New Registration" and create an account with your valid email address and mobile number. Verify both via OTP.

Fill Application Form
  • Personal details – name, date of birth, gender, category, domicile
  • Academic details – diploma / B.Sc. marks, institution, year of passing
  • Course selection – B.Tech or B.Pharmacy, preferred branches
  • Examination centre – select preferred exam city
Upload Documents
  • Passport-size photograph (JPG, max 50 KB)
  • Signature (JPG, max 20 KB)
  • Diploma / B.Sc. marksheet and certificate
  • Domicile certificate & category certificate (if applicable)
Pay Application Fee

Pay online via Debit / Credit Card, Net Banking, or UPI. General category – ₹1000 | Reserved categories – ₹500 (subject to change per official notification). Fee is non-refundable.

Submit & Download Confirmation

Review all details carefully, submit the form, and download the confirmation page. Keep login credentials safe for future use.

Download Admit Card

Admit cards are released approximately 1 week before the exam. Download and print it — carry it along with a valid photo ID on exam day.

💡 Keep all original documents ready for verification during counselling. Check the official website regularly for updates and correction window notifications.

Preparation Tips

Start Early & Create a Schedule

Plan a 2–3 month study schedule with daily targets for theory revision, PYQ solving and mock tests. Stick to it consistently.

Focus on Fundamentals

Revise core Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry from your HSBTE-prescribed diploma textbooks. Strong fundamentals carry the most marks.

Practice Previous Year Papers

Solve at least 5 years of LEET PYQ under timed conditions. This is the most accurate predictor of your actual exam performance.

Take Regular Mock Tests

Simulate exam-day conditions weekly — 90 questions in 90 minutes, no phone. Track speed and accuracy improvements each time.

Revise Formulas & Concepts

Maintain a formula sheet for Mathematics and Physics. Review it daily in the final 2 weeks before the exam.

Stay Updated & Manage Health

Stay updated with exam notifications. Maintain good health, sleep well and manage stress — exam-day performance depends on it.

Counselling Process

The counselling process for HSBTE LEET is conducted online in multiple rounds to ensure fair and transparent seat allocation based on merit and candidate preferences.

Online Registration for Counselling
  • After result declaration, login with your LEET application credentials
  • Fill counselling registration form and pay counselling fee (if applicable)
  • Download counselling registration slip
Choice Filling
  • Browse available colleges and branches on the portal
  • Select and arrange choices in order of preference (most preferred first)
  • You can typically add 50–100 choices — fill as many as possible
Choice Locking

Review all selected choices carefully and lock before the deadline. Once locked, choices cannot be modified. Download the locked-choices confirmation.

Seat Allotment
  • Based on LEET rank / merit, locked preferences, category and seat availability
  • Allotment results published online — download seat allotment letter
  • Multiple rounds: Round 1 → Round 2 → Spot Counselling (if seats remain)
Document Verification
  • Report to designated verification centre with all original documents
  • LEET Admit Card, Rank Card, Seat Allotment Letter
  • Diploma / B.Sc. marksheets, 10th certificate, domicile, category certificate, photo ID
Fee Payment & Reporting to College

Pay admission fee online or at the college. Report on the specified date, submit all documents and complete admission formalities.

💡 Participate in all counselling rounds to maximise your chances. If you don't report after seat allotment you may lose the seat. You can participate in the next round even after taking admission (upgrade option).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is HSBTE LEET?▼
HSBTE LEET (Lateral Entry Engineering Test) is the state-level online entrance test conducted by HSTES for diploma holders and B.Sc. graduates who want direct admission into the 2nd year (3rd semester) of B.Tech or B.Pharmacy in Haryana colleges — skipping Year 1 entirely.
When will the LEET 2026 notification and exam date be released?▼
The official LEET 2026 notification and exam date will be released by HSTES on its website. Based on previous years, the notification usually comes in the first half of the year (May–June), followed by the exam a few weeks later. Always verify the latest schedule on the official HSTES portal.
Is there negative marking in HSBTE LEET?▼
No — there is no negative marking in the B.Tech LEET as per the official 2025–26 scheme. Each correct answer carries +1 mark and incorrect answers carry no penalty. Always confirm this from the latest official brochure before the exam.
How can I download LEET previous year question papers and sample papers?▼
You can download Haryana LEET previous year question papers and sample papers from HSBTE PYQ — use the LEET hub page, the LEET sample paper page, and the HSBTE PYQ section, which provide organised links to branch-wise and course-wise question papers for both B.Tech and B.Pharmacy.
What is the exam pattern for HSBTE LEET?▼
B.Tech LEET: 90 MCQs in 90 minutes, 90 marks total, no negative marking, conducted online. The paper has 4 sections — Basic Sciences (25 marks), Electronics Stream (25 marks), Mechanical Stream (20 marks), and Other Streams (20 marks). B.Pharmacy LEET pattern is as per the separate OCET brochure.
How is counselling done for HSBTE LEET?▼
After result declaration, eligible candidates register for online counselling through the HSTES portal. The process involves registration, document verification, choice filling (50–100 choices), choice locking, seat allotment (multiple rounds), and final reporting to the allotted college. Fill as many choices as possible to maximise your chances.
How many months of preparation is enough?▼
With focused and disciplined preparation — daily study, PYQ solving and regular mock tests — 2 to 3 months is usually sufficient for HSBTE LEET. If you start earlier, use extra time for deeper concept revision and more test cycles.
⚠️ Always verify dates, eligibility & rules from the official HSTES website before applying.
