Understanding LEET Cutoff

The cutoff marks for HSBTE LEET vary each year based on several factors:

  • Number of applicants
  • Difficulty level of the exam
  • Number of available seats
  • Performance of candidates
  • Category (General, SC, ST, OBC)
  • Branch preference

Cutoff Trends

Cutoff marks are typically higher for popular branches like Computer Science Engineering and lower for less preferred branches. General category candidates usually need higher marks compared to reserved category candidates.

Note: Exact cutoff marks will be updated after the official announcement. Check the official HSBTE website and the cutoff portal for the latest cutoff information.
