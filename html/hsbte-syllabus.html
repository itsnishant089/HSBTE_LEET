About HSBTE Diploma Syllabus 2025

The Haryana State Board of Technical Education (HSBTE) has released updated diploma syllabi effective from August 2024 for all branches across its affiliated polytechnic colleges in Haryana. These revised curricula are aligned with the latest industry requirements and AICTE norms, ensuring that students receive practical, job-ready education over the 3-year (6-semester) program.

Each syllabus PDF linked above is the official document released by HSBTE. It includes course objectives, teaching hours, examination scheme, theory topics, and practical lab content for every semester. Students are advised to download the syllabus for their specific branch at the start of each academic year.

What the Syllabus Covers

  • Theory Subjects - Core engineering/management topics with detailed chapter-wise breakdown
  • Practical Sessions - Lab exercises, experiments, and hands-on training hours per subject
  • Internal Assessment - Sessional exams, assignments, and continuous evaluation criteria
  • Industrial Training - Summer/winter training guidelines integrated into the curriculum
  • Project Work - Final year project guidelines and evaluation scheme

How to Use the Syllabus Effectively

  • Download your branch PDF and mark high-weightage topics before exam season
  • Cross-reference syllabus topics with HSBTE Previous Year Question Papers to identify frequently asked questions
  • Use the practical list to prepare lab viva answers in advance
  • Diploma students planning lateral entry should note that LEET exam topics directly overlap with 1st and 2nd year diploma syllabus - especially Mathematics, Physics, and branch core subjects

Diploma Syllabus & Haryana LEET Connection

If you are a diploma student planning to appear for Haryana LEET 2026, your diploma syllabus is your primary preparation resource. The LEET entrance exam tests diploma-level concepts in:

  • Mathematics (from 1st & 2nd year diploma common subjects)
  • Physics fundamentals (Engineering Physics)
  • Branch-specific subjects (from your diploma core papers)
  • Basic Electronics, Electrical, and Computer concepts (for Sections B-D)

Revising your branch syllabus thoroughly gives you a significant advantage in the LEET exam. Combine this with LEET PYQ practice and the LEET preparation guide for best results.

Frequently Asked Questions - HSBTE Syllabus

Where can I download the official HSBTE diploma syllabus PDF? ?
You can download the official HSBTE diploma syllabus PDFs directly from this page - each branch card above links to the official document released by HSBTE. The syllabus is effective from August 2024 (session 2024-25 onwards).
Is the HSBTE syllabus the same for all polytechnic colleges in Haryana? ?
Yes. All HSBTE-affiliated government and private polytechnic colleges in Haryana follow the same prescribed syllabus issued by the board. The curriculum applies uniformly across all institutes for their respective diploma programs.
Is the HSBTE 2024 revised syllabus applicable for LEET preparation? ?
Yes. The Haryana LEET exam tests concepts from the first two years of diploma education. The 2024-25 revised HSBTE syllabus is highly relevant for LEET 2026 preparation, particularly for branch-specific topics and basic sciences (Mathematics, Physics).
How many branches does HSBTE offer diploma programs in? ?
HSBTE offers diploma programs in over 25 branches including Computer Engineering, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, ECE, AI & ML, Automobile, Chemical, Ceramic, Food Technology, Pharmacy (D.Pharm), Medical Lab Technology, and many more. All branch syllabi are available on this page.
After diploma, can I directly join BTech 2nd year in Haryana? ?
Yes! Diploma holders can apply for Haryana LEET (Lateral Entry Entrance Test) and get direct admission to the 2nd year of B.Tech programs in Haryana colleges. This saves one year and opens up better career opportunities with a degree-level qualification.
