Haryana LEET 2026 Overview

Haryana LEET 2026 (Lateral Entry Engineering Test) is conducted by the Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES) as a common online Computer-Based Test (CBT / OCET) for diploma holders and B.Sc. graduates who want direct admission into the 2nd year (3rd semester) of B.Tech or B.Pharmacy programmes in Haryana engineering and pharmacy colleges.

Eligibility for Haryana LEET 2026

Exact eligibility will be defined in the official HSTES brochure. Based on previous years, the following general conditions apply:

🔧 B.Tech LEET – Eligibility Criteria

  • ✓3-Year Diploma in Engineering/Technology: Must have passed in a relevant branch from a recognized board (HSBTE or equivalent). Minimum aggregate percentage as notified by HSTES (typically 45% for General, 40% for SC/BC candidates of Haryana).
  • ✓B.Sc. Graduates (where permitted): Candidates with B.Sc. degree with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM) may be eligible for specific B.Tech branches as per HSTES rules — confirm from official 2026 brochure.
  • ✓Domicile Requirement: Candidates must fulfil Haryana domicile and residency conditions as specified in the HSTES brochure for state quota seats.
  • ✓Age Limit: No specific upper age limit has been prescribed in previous years — verify in the official 2026 brochure.
  • ✓Reservation: SC / BC / EWS and other reserved categories get relaxation in minimum qualifying marks as per Haryana Government norms.
  • ✓Branch Alignment: Diploma branch should correspond to the B.Tech branch being applied for, as per HSTES rules.

💊 B.Pharmacy LEET – Eligibility Criteria

  • ✓D.Pharmacy (Diploma in Pharmacy): Must have passed from an institute approved by the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI). Minimum percentage as per HSTES brochure (typically 45% for General, 40% for SC/BC of Haryana).
  • ✓State Pharmacy Council Registration: Eligible for registration or already registered with the Haryana/Punjab State Pharmacy Council.
  • ✓Domicile: Haryana domicile required for state category seats.
  • ✓Reservation: Category-wise relaxation as per Haryana Government norms. Confirm from the latest B.Pharmacy LEET prospectus on HSTES portal.
📌 Before applying, always confirm the final eligibility rules in the Haryana LEET 2026 information brochure on the official HSTES website (hstes.org.in).

Preparation Strategy for Haryana LEET 2026

For a structured plan, follow the detailed LEET preparation guide. Here is a proven step-by-step framework:

Analyse the Syllabus & PYQ First

Download the HSTES syllabus. Cross-reference with 3–5 years of PYQ to find highest-weightage topics. Focus on Section A (Basic Sciences – 25 marks) as it is common for all streams.

Create a 2–3 Month Study Schedule

Divide into Phase 1 (concept revision), Phase 2 (PYQ practice), Phase 3 (online mock tests + revision). Target at least 3–4 hours of focused study daily.

Revise Diploma Subjects Thoroughly

Revise diploma subjects focusing on frequently asked topics from your HSBTE-prescribed textbooks. Pay extra attention to Mathematics (8 Qs) and Physics (8 Qs) — highest in Section A.

Practice Online Mock Tests

Since LEET is an online CBT, practice on screen. Set a 90-minute timer, attempt 90 questions online, then self-evaluate. Track accuracy and speed across attempts.

Attempt ALL Questions – No Negative Marking

There is zero negative marking — never leave any question blank. If unsure, eliminate wrong options and make your best guess. This alone can improve your score significantly.

Use Free Online Resources

ChatGPT, Google Gemini and YouTube are excellent free resources. There are no specific books for LEET — avoid wasting money. Focus revision time on weak topics using these free tools.

Counselling, Colleges & Cutoff for 2026

After the Haryana LEET 2026 result, HSTES will conduct online centralised counselling for seat allotment in participating engineering and pharmacy colleges across Haryana.

  1. Counselling Registration & Fee Payment on official HSTES portal (hstes.org.in / techadmissionshry.gov.in)
  2. Online Document Verification – upload diploma certificate, marksheets, domicile, category certificate, class 10 certificate
  3. Choice Filling & Locking – fill college and branch preferences in priority order. Lock choices before deadline.
  4. Seat Allotment – Round 1, Round 2, Stray Vacancy Round. Pay seat acceptance fee to confirm.
  5. Document Verification at Allotted College – physical verification of original documents
  6. Fee Deposit & Admission Confirmation – pay college fee to secure your seat

Read the LEET counselling guide for the step-by-step process. Check previous year cutoff to estimate your target rank. Fill as many college choices as possible — more choices = higher chance of a good allotment.

Quick FAQ – Haryana LEET 2026

Is Haryana LEET 2026 a pen-paper exam or online?▼
Haryana LEET 2026 is an Online Computer-Based Test (CBT), also called OCET. It is NOT a pen-paper exam. You will sit at a computer terminal and answer 90 MCQs in 90 minutes at designated exam centres across Haryana.
Is there negative marking in Haryana LEET 2026?▼
No, there is NO negative marking in Haryana LEET 2026. Each correct answer gives +1 mark and wrong answers have zero deduction. You should attempt all 90 questions — never leave any blank!
What is the section-wise marks distribution for B.Tech LEET?▼
B.Tech LEET has 4 sections: Section A – Basic Sciences (25 marks, common for all streams), Section B – Electronics/CSE/IT Stream (25 marks), Section C – Mechanical Stream (20 marks), Section D – Civil/Textile/Chemical and Other Streams (20 marks). Total = 90 marks.
Is Haryana LEET 2026 only for diploma holders?▼
Mainly yes — it is for diploma holders in relevant branches. In some cases B.Sc. graduates (with PCM subjects) are also eligible for B.Tech LEET. For B.Pharmacy LEET, D.Pharm holders are eligible. Always confirm from the official HSTES brochure.
Where can I get the official Haryana LEET 2026 notification?▼
The official LEET 2026 notification will be available on the HSTES website (hstes.org.in) and the admissions portal (techadmissionshry.gov.in). Use this page only as a student-friendly reference guide. The official brochure will have final dates, rules and eligibility criteria.
How many months are enough for Haryana LEET 2026 preparation?▼
With focused study and proper use of PYQ, 2–3 months of disciplined preparation is sufficient. Start with Section A (Basic Sciences — Math and Physics are highest weightage). Since there is no negative marking, practice attempting all questions in online mock tests.
How can I download Haryana LEET PYQ and sample papers?▼
Download free Haryana LEET PYQ and sample papers from HSBTE PYQ. Use the Haryana LEET hub page, the LEET sample paper page, and the HSBTE PYQ section for organised links to branch-wise and year-wise question papers. All resources are free.
How is counselling done for Haryana LEET 2026?▼
After the Haryana LEET 2026 result, candidates register for online counselling on the HSTES portal. Steps: registration & fee, document verification, choice filling & locking, seat allotment (multiple rounds), report to allotted college. Fill maximum choices for best allotment chances.
