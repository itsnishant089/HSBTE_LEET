Official Pattern vs This Guide

Question Paper Structure (B.Tech LEET - as per official scheme)

According to the B.Tech (LEET) Syllabus 2027-26, the Haryana LEET paper for B.Tech lateral entry has the following pattern:

  • Mode of Examination: Common Online Entrance Test (computer-based)
  • Question Type: Objective Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
  • Total Questions: 90
  • Total Marks: 90 (1 mark for each question)
  • Exam Duration: 90 minutes
  • Marking Scheme: +1 for every correct answer, no negative marking

Section-wise Distribution

  • Section A - Basic Sciences (25 questions / 25 marks)
    • Mathematics - 8 questions
    • Physics - 8 questions
    • Chemistry - 3 questions
    • Communication Skills - 3 questions
    • General Awareness - 3 questions
  • Section B - Electronics Stream Courses (25 questions / 25 marks)
    • Elements of Electrical Engineering - 9 questions
    • Elements of Electronics Engineering - 8 questions
    • Elements of Computer Engineering - 8 questions
  • Section C - Mechanical Stream Courses (20 questions / 20 marks)
    • Elements of Mechanical Engineering - 8 questions
    • Elements of Production Engineering / Manufacturing Processes - 6 questions
    • Automobile Engineering - 6 questions
  • Section D - Other Engineering Streams (20 questions / 20 marks)
    • Civil Engineering Courses - 5 questions
    • Textile Engineering Courses - 5 questions
    • Chemical / Printing Technology - 2 questions
    • Ceramic Engineering - 2 questions
    • Food Technology - 2 questions
    • Other streams (Agriculture, Architecture, Fashion Design / Technology etc.) - 4 questions

This structure is taken directly from the official scheme of entrance test; if HSTES issues any updated pattern for 2027, follow the latest brochure.

How to Use Exam Pattern for Preparation

  • Simulate real exam conditions when solving Haryana LEET sample papers or LEET PYQ.
  • Allocate time per section based on expected weightage - don-t spend too long on any single question.

  • Review your performance after every mock to adjust speed and accuracy.
