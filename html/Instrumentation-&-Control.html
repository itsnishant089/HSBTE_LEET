About Instrumentation & Control

Instrumentation & Control Engineering focuses on measurement and automatic control of industrial processes. The HSBTE diploma trains students to work with sensors, transmitters, controllers, PLCs, and industrial automation systems.

Students learn how physical variables like temperature, pressure, flow, and level are measured and controlled in industries such as power plants, chemical plants, manufacturing, and automation systems.

What Students Learn

  • Industrial measurement techniques
  • Sensors and transducers
  • Process instrumentation
  • Control systems fundamentals
  • PLC programming basics
  • SCADA and automation systems
  • Signal conditioning and calibration
  • Industrial electronics
  • Process control loops
  • Instrumentation maintenance

Career Opportunities

  • Instrumentation Technician
  • Control System Technician
  • Automation Technician
  • PLC / SCADA Technician
  • Calibration Technician
  • Process Plant Technician
  • Industrial Maintenance Technician
  • Instrumentation Service Engineer

Why Choose Instrumentation & Control

Modern industries rely heavily on automation and process control. Skilled instrumentation professionals are essential in power, oil & gas, manufacturing, and chemical industries. This branch offers strong industrial demand and automation-focused careers.

Real-World Applications

  • Power plant control systems
  • Process industry automation
  • Manufacturing automation lines
  • Chemical plant monitoring systems
  • Water treatment instrumentation
  • Industrial process control loops
  • Plant safety monitoring systems
  • Building automation systems

Higher Education After This Diploma

After Instrumentation & Control diploma, students can pursue B.Tech in Instrumentation, Electronics, Electrical, or Automation Engineering through LEET. They can specialise in control systems, robotics, or industrial automation.
