Frequently Asked Questions

What is LEET Counseling Help?
Our counseling experts analyze your LEET rank, category, diploma branch, preferred colleges/branches and location preferences to provide personalized recommendations. Every suggestion explains why a college or branch fits your profile so you can fill safer and smarter choices during HSTES counselling.
How will I receive the response?
After you submit your request, our admin reviews it within 24-48 hours and sends detailed suggestions to your dashboard at user-counseling.
Can I submit multiple requests?
Yes! You can submit multiple requests — for example after each counseling round if your rank situation changes.
