The exact and final syllabus for Haryana LEET 2026 is always provided in the official information brochure released by HSTES. The topics given below are based on the usual pattern followed in recent years and on diploma-level subjects. Always cross-check with the latest brochure before final preparation.

B.Tech LEET – Expected Syllabus Structure

The Haryana LEET 2026 syllabus for B.Tech lateral entry usually covers the following broad areas:

  • Mathematics (diploma / class 11–12 level)
  • Physics fundamentals
  • Basic Engineering & Computer concepts
  • Branch-specific diploma subjects

Mathematics

  • Algebra – sets, relations, functions, complex numbers, sequences and series
  • Matrices and Determinants – rank, inverse, system of linear equations
  • Differential Calculus – limits, continuity, differentiation, applications
  • Integral Calculus – definite/indefinite integrals, area under curves
  • Differential Equations – first order, basic applications
  • Coordinate Geometry – straight lines, circles, conic sections
  • Vector Algebra and 3D Geometry – basic operations, direction cosines
  • Probability & Statistics – basic probability, mean, variance
  • Trigonometry – identities, equations, inverse trigonometric functions

Physics

  • Units & Measurements, Kinematics, Newton’s Laws of Motion
  • Work, Energy & Power, Rotational Motion
  • Properties of Matter – elasticity, surface tension, viscosity
  • Heat & Thermodynamics – laws, heat transfer, kinetic theory
  • Waves & Oscillations, Sound
  • Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetism
  • Electromagnetic Induction, AC circuits
  • Optics – reflection, refraction, lenses, wave optics (basics)
  • Modern Physics – atoms, nuclei, semiconductors (intro level)

Basic Engineering / Computer Fundamentals

  • Engineering Drawing & Basics of Engineering Graphics (conceptual)
  • Basic Electrical & Electronics (Ohm’s law, circuits, diodes, transistors)
  • Computer Fundamentals – hardware, software, OS, number systems
  • Programming Fundamentals (C / basic logic) – variables, loops, functions

Branch-Specific Diploma Subjects

The branch-specific portion depends on your diploma branch (CSE, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, ECE etc.). Focus on core subjects like:

  • Computer: Programming in C/C++, Data Structures, DBMS basics, Digital Electronics
  • Mechanical: Strength of Materials, Theory of Machines, Thermodynamics, Manufacturing
  • Civil: Strength of Materials, Surveying, Fluid Mechanics, Building Construction
  • Electrical: Network Analysis, Machines, Power Systems (basics), Measurements
  • ECE: Analog & Digital Electronics, Communication Systems (intro), Networks

B.Pharmacy LEET – Broad Syllabus Areas

For B.Pharmacy lateral entry through Haryana LEET, the expected syllabus generally includes diploma-level pharmacy subjects:

  • Pharmaceutics: dosage forms, drug delivery systems, tablets, capsules, ointments, emulsions, suspensions
  • Pharmaceutical Chemistry: organic chemistry basics, medicinal chemistry fundamentals, drug classification
  • Pharmacology: general pharmacology, autonomic nervous system, cardiovascular drugs, CNS drugs
  • Pharmacognosy: crude drugs, sources, classification, active constituents
  • Pharmaceutical Analysis: titrimetric analysis, instrumentation fundamentals
  • Human Anatomy & Physiology: body systems, organs, basic functions
