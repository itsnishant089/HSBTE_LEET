How Does the HSTES Centralized Counselling & Choice Filling Work?

Haryana lateral entry seats are allotted via centralized online counselling conducted over two primary rounds on techadmissionshry.gov.in.

Step 01
May – June

Official Admission Notification

HSTES releases the detailed Information Brochure & Prospectus outlining eligibility criteria, seat matrices, and counselling rules on hstes.org.in.

Action: Download official brochure & review seat matrix
Step 02
June – July

Online Registration & Application

Aspirants register on techadmissionshry.gov.in, validate mandatory Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP ID), upload marks, and submit fees (₹1,000 General / ₹700 Reserved).

Action: Fill application accurately & link PPP ID
Step 03
July

Document Verification

Online or designated nodal polytechnic verification of diploma transcripts, category certificates (SC/BCA/BCB/EWS), Haryana resident proof, and family ID.

Action: Ensure certificates are valid for current financial year
Step 04
Late July – Early August

Entrance Exam / Merit Preparation

HSTES calculates the inter-se merit based on qualifying diploma examination or conduct of online OCET with tie-breaking criteria applied transparently.

Action: Check provisional merit & submit grievances if any
Step 05
August

Merit Rank Declaration

Final Inter-Se Merit List and individual rank cards are published online on techadmissionshry.gov.in. The unique LEET rank determines counselling priority.

Action: Download & print official HSTES Rank Card
Step 06
August – September (2 Rounds)

Counselling & Choice Locking

Candidates log in to fill and lock their preferred colleges and branches in order of priority. Choice locking is mandatory before the countdown timer expires.

Action: Prioritize top university departments & lock choices
Step 07
September

Seat Allotment & Physical Reporting

Provisional seat allotment letters are generated. Candidates report physically to the allotted college for document verification and semester fee payment.

Action: Carry original documents + 2 sets of attested photocopies

Who Conducts What: HSBTE (Board) vs HSTES (Admission Society)

The term "HSBTE LEET" is widely searched by polytechnic students across Haryana, but there is an essential administrative distinction that every candidate must understand:

HSBTE (The Diploma Board)

Haryana State Board of Technical Education: Administers the 3-Year Polytechnic Diploma curriculum, sets semester exams, manages polytechnic results, and issues diploma certificates.

Official Portal: hsbte.org.in

HSTES (The Admission Society)

Haryana State Technical Education Society: Releases the official LEET brochure, conducts the entrance examination (OCET) / merit ranking, and manages centralized online counselling on techadmissionshry.gov.in.

Official Portal: techadmissionshry.gov.in

Mandatory for Haryana Residents: Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP ID)

As per Haryana State Government guidelines, entering a valid 8-digit Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID) is mandatory for candidates claiming the 85% Haryana State Quota, caste reservation (SC / Deprived SC / BCA / BCB), Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW), or EWS benefits.

  • Candidate and parent details, caste status, and verified annual family income are fetched directly from the CRID database.
  • All India Category (15% Quota): Candidates from outside Haryana can register without a PPP ID using standard Aadhaar or general identity credentials.
  • Ensure your Family ID reflects your current active mobile number for receiving OTP verification during registration.

Mandatory Document Checklist for Verification & Reporting

Keep both original documents and at least two self-attested photocopy sets ready before online document verification and physical institute reporting:

1. Diploma / Degree Marksheets

All semester marksheets or consolidated transcript issued by HSBTE or recognized university.

2. Provisional / Final Certificate

Passing or provisional certificate from the State Board of Technical Education.

3. Matriculation (10th) Certificate

Official certificate as proof of candidate's Date of Birth and parentage.

4. Haryana Resident Certificate

Mandatory for claiming 85% Haryana State Quota seats.

5. Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID)

Mandatory for Haryana residents for auto-fetching verified domicile, caste, and income.

6. Category Certificate (SC/BCA/BCB/EWS)

Issued by competent revenue authority in Haryana in current financial year format.

7. Latest Income Certificate

Mandatory for claiming Non-Creamy Layer benefits for BCA/BCB and fee waivers under TFW.

8. Character Certificate

From the Head of the polytechnic institute last attended.

9. Medical Fitness Certificate

From a registered medical practitioner as per the format specified in HSTES brochure.

10. Anti-Ragging Undertakings

Affidavits submitted online or signed at the time of physical reporting.

Smart Choice Filling Strategy for Lateral Entry

Because lateral entry seats are competitive, following a calculated choice-filling order significantly maximizes your chances of securing a top government university seat:

1. Strict Priority Order

Fill top-tier state universities first (e.g., J.C. Bose UST YMCA Faridabad, DCRUST Murthal, UIET KUK, GJUS&T Hisar) even if your rank is moderate. The HSTES allotment algorithm strictly evaluates choices from top to bottom.

2. Fill Maximum Choices

Never restrict your list to only 2 or 3 colleges. Add plenty of realistic backup branches and autonomous institutes to ensure you do not get eliminated from Round 1 allotment.

3. Mandatory Choice Locking

HSTES requires candidates to explicitly lock their choices before the countdown timer hits zero. Unlocked choices might reset or get invalidated during final seat processing.

Counselling Overview - B.Tech LEET

After declaration of the Haryana LEET 2027 result for B.Tech, eligible diploma / B.Sc. candidates must participate in online counselling for engineering seats. The process is fully merit-cum-preference based.

The broad steps are:

  1. Online registration & counselling fee payment
  2. Choice filling & locking of B.Tech colleges / branches
  3. Seat allotment result
  4. Payment of seat acceptance fee (if applicable)
  5. Reporting to allotted institute with documents
  6. Subsequent rounds / upgradation if conducted

Counselling Overview - B.Pharmacy LEET

For B.Pharmacy LEET (lateral entry after D.Pharm), counselling is also done through the HSTES portal but only for institutes and seats offering B.Pharm LEET. The broad steps are similar, but your choices and cutoff trends will be pharmacy-specific.

Key idea: you will still register, fill choices, get seat allotment and report to the pharmacy college within the notified dates.

Step 1 - Online Registration & Fee Payment

  • Visit the HSTES counselling portal after the schedule is announced.
  • Click on the Haryana LEET 2027 counselling link and create your account using basic details and LEET roll number.
  • Pay the counselling fee online using the permitted payment modes.
  • Keep the payment receipt / transaction ID safe - it may be needed later.

Step 2 - Choice Filling & Locking

  • Log in to the portal and view the list of participating colleges and available branches.
  • Arrange choices in your preferred order - you can add many options to increase chances of seat allotment.
  • Use previous year Haryana LEET cutoff to make realistic choices based on your expected rank.
  • Lock your choices before the deadline. If you forget to lock, the last saved list may be considered - but do not depend on this.

Step 3 - Seat Allotment

  • HSTES will publish the seat allotment result on the portal for each counselling round.
  • Login using your credentials to see allotted college and branch.
  • Download the provisional allotment letter and read the reporting instructions carefully.

Step 4 - Reporting to Allotted College

Candidates must report to the allotted institute within the given time period with all required documents.

Important Documents (Typical List)

  • Haryana LEET 2027 admit card & result/score card (when released)
  • Diploma / D.Pharmacy mark sheets and provisional/final certificate
  • 10th and 12th mark sheets & certificates (if required)
  • Category, domicile, income and other reservation certificates (if applicable)
  • Passport size photographs
  • Photo ID proof (Aadhaar / other accepted ID)
  • Fee as per institute-s instructions

Carry both original and photocopies as mentioned in the official notice.

Use the admission document checklist for reporting and the OCET document page if you still need the exam-day list. For better choice filling, also compare seats on the branch-wise intake page.

Counselling Tips

  • Read every official notification carefully - dates change frequently.
  • Prepare a list of preferred colleges in advance - see Haryana LEET colleges overview.
  • Fill a good number of choices including safe options - not only top colleges.
  • Keep track of each counselling round and upgradation rules.
  • Do not wait till the last day for registration, fee payment or reporting.
