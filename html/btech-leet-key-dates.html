Important Admission Notes

The B.Tech LEET 2027-27 schedule is tentative. HSTES may revise any date, so check the official portal regularly before relying on a single screenshot or social post.
  • The number of OCET exam days depends on the total number of candidates.
  • PPP ID is mandatory for Haryana candidates at the time of registration and detail confirmation.
  • Registration fee is Rs. 1000 for General category and Rs. 700 for reserved categories.
  • Reserved categories listed in the shared schedule are SC, SCD, BC, PH, FF, ESM, KM, Girls and EWS.
  • Admission is based on the OCET-2027 inter-se-rank for lateral entry to B.E./B.Tech.
