Haryana LEET 2026 Overview

Haryana LEET 2026 (Lateral Entry Engineering Test) is conducted by HSTES for diploma holders and B.Sc. graduates who want direct admission into the 2nd year of B.Tech or B.Pharmacy programmes in Haryana engineering and pharmacy colleges.

  • Exam Name: Haryana LEET 2026
  • Conducting Body: Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES)
  • Courses: B.Tech (Lateral Entry), B.Pharmacy (Lateral Entry)
  • Admission Type: Direct admission into 2nd year (3rd semester)
  • Mode of Exam: As per latest HSTES notification

Always check the latest official brochure and notifications on the HSTES website for final information.

Eligibility for Haryana LEET 2026

Exact eligibility will be defined in the official brochure. Typical eligibility conditions (based on previous years) include:

  • Diploma in Engineering / Technology in a relevant branch from a recognized board with required minimum aggregate percentage.
  • Or B.Sc. degree with appropriate combination of subjects as per HSTES rules.
  • Fulfilment of age, domicile and reservation criteria defined by HSTES.

Before applying, always confirm the final eligibility rules in the Haryana LEET 2026 information brochure on the official HSTES website.
