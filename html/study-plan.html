No Negative Marking
Attempt all 90 questions. Never leave any question blank. Even a random guess gives a 25% chance of getting it right.

Time Management
90 questions in 90 minutes = 1 min per question. If stuck on a question for more than 45 sec, mark and move on. Return later.
