Haryana LEET 2026 Overview

Haryana LEET 2026 (Lateral Entry Engineering Test) is conducted by the Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES) for diploma holders and B.Sc. graduates who want direct admission into the 2nd year (3rd semester) of B.Tech or B.Pharmacy programmes in Haryana engineering and pharmacy colleges. It is one of the most student-friendly lateral entry routes in North India, letting diploma holders bypass Year 1 and join degree programmes directly.

Eligibility for Haryana LEET 2026

Exact eligibility will be defined in the official HSTES brochure. Based on previous years, the following general conditions apply:

  • ✓Diploma Holders (B.Tech LEET): Must have passed Diploma in Engineering / Technology in a relevant branch from a recognized board with the required minimum aggregate percentage (typically 45%–60% depending on category).
  • ✓B.Sc. Graduates (B.Tech LEET): Candidates with a B.Sc. degree with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM) or equivalent combination may be eligible for specific B.Tech branches as per HSTES rules.
  • ✓Diploma Holders (B.Pharmacy LEET): Must hold a Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm) from a recognized institution under Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) norms.
  • ✓Domicile Requirement: Candidates must fulfil Haryana domicile and residency conditions as specified by HSTES for state quota seats.
  • ✓Age Limit: No specific upper age limit has been prescribed in previous years — verify this in the official 2026 brochure.
  • ✓Reservation: SC / BC / EWS and other reserved categories get relaxation in minimum qualifying marks as per Haryana Government norms.
📌 Before applying, always confirm the final eligibility rules in the Haryana LEET 2026 information brochure on the official HSTES website.

Preparation Strategy for Haryana LEET 2026

For a structured plan, follow the detailed LEET preparation guide. Here is a proven step-by-step framework:

Analyse the Syllabus & PYQ First

Download the HSTES syllabus. Cross-reference with 3–5 years of PYQ to find the highest-weightage topics. Build your study plan around those — not the entire textbook.

Create a 2–3 Month Study Schedule

Divide into Phase 1 (concept revision), Phase 2 (PYQ practice), Phase 3 (mock tests + revision). Stick to daily targets of at least 3–4 hours of focused study.

Revise Diploma Subjects Thoroughly

Revise diploma subjects thoroughly, focusing on frequently asked topics from your HSBTE-prescribed textbooks and notes. Pay extra attention to Mathematics, Physics and branch-specific subjects.

Solve PYQ Under Exam Conditions

Set a 3-hour timer, attempt the full paper, then self-evaluate. Track accuracy and speed across multiple attempts — this is the best predictor of your actual performance.

Take Regular Mock Tests

Take regular mock tests to improve speed and accuracy. Simulate exam-day conditions — no phone, proper timing and honest self-assessment in the final month.

Use Online Resources Wisely

Use online resources (videos, notes) only to clarify concepts — avoid random browsing. Invest revision time in weak topics, not ones you already know well.

Counselling, Colleges & Cutoff for 2026

After the Haryana LEET 2026 result, HSTES will conduct online centralised counselling for seat allotment in participating engineering and pharmacy colleges across Haryana.

  1. Counselling Registration & Fee Payment on HSTES portal
  2. Online Document Verification
  3. Choice Filling & Locking of preferred Colleges / Branches
  4. Seat Allotment – Round 1, Round 2, Stray Vacancy Round
  5. Document Verification at Allotted College
  6. Fee Deposit & Admission Confirmation

Read the LEET counselling guide for step-by-step process. Check previous year cutoff to estimate your target rank and possible colleges. Use the official HSTES website for latest college lists, seat matrix and category-wise rules.

Cutoff for Haryana LEET 2026 will depend on factors like number of applicants, difficulty of the paper and total available seats. Fill as many college choices as possible — more choices = higher chance of a good allotment.

Quick FAQ – Haryana LEET 2026

Is Haryana LEET 2026 only for diploma holders?▼
Mainly yes — it is for diploma holders in relevant branches and in some cases B.Sc. graduates, as per official eligibility rules. For B.Pharmacy LEET, D.Pharm holders are eligible. Always confirm from the official HSTES brochure.
Where can I get the official Haryana LEET 2026 notification?▼
On the official HSTES website (hstes.org.in). Use this page only as a student-friendly reference guide. The official brochure will have final dates, rules and eligibility criteria.
How many months are enough for Haryana LEET 2026 preparation?▼
With focused study and proper use of PYQ, 2–3 months of disciplined preparation is usually sufficient. If you start earlier, use extra time for deeper concept revision and more mock test cycles.
When will Haryana LEET 2026 notification and exam date be released?▼
The official Haryana LEET 2026 notification and exam date will be released by HSTES on its website. Based on previous years, the notification usually comes in the first half of the year, followed by the exam a few weeks later. Always verify the latest schedule on the official HSTES portal.
How can I download Haryana LEET PYQ and sample papers?▼
You can download Haryana LEET previous year question papers and sample papers from HSBTE PYQ. Use the Haryana LEET hub page, the LEET sample paper page, and the HSBTE PYQ section, which provide organised links to branch-wise and course-wise question papers.
How is counselling done for Haryana LEET 2026?▼
After declaration of the Haryana LEET 2026 result, eligible candidates must register for online counselling through the HSTES portal. The process involves registration, document verification, choice filling, seat allotment (multiple rounds) and fee payment at the allotted college. The LEET counselling page on this site explains the step-by-step process.
Is there negative marking in Haryana LEET 2026?▼
The negative marking scheme is defined in the official HSTES notification. In previous years there has been no negative marking or minimal deduction. Always confirm the exact marking scheme from the official 2026 brochure before exam day.
