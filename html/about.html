About HSBTELEET.COM

An independent student-driven educational repository dedicated to archiving HSBTE Diploma question papers and simplifying Haryana Lateral Entry Entrance Test (LEET) preparation.

Our Purpose & Resources

HSBTELEET.COM was founded by students to solve two widespread problems faced by technical students across Haryana: the unavailability of categorized previous semester diploma question papers, and the lack of a centralized, well-structured guide for diploma-to-degree lateral entry (B.Tech LEET) admissions.

280+ HSBTE PYQs

Carefully archived semester exams for Computer Science, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, and Electronics engineering with clean PDF access.

Haryana LEET Pillar

Up-to-date guidance on Haryana B.Tech LEET eligibility, syllabus breakdown, exam pattern, seat intake, and previous years' opening/closing ranks.

Preparation Material

Subject-wise weightage, high-scoring syllabus topics, sample mock papers, and diploma math/science preparation strategy.

Transparent Editorial

Every admission guideline, cutoff table, and eligibility criterion is verified against official state brochures before publication.

Editorial Standards & Verification Policy

Maintaining academic trust and accuracy is our top priority. We adhere to the following principles across all content published on HSBTELEET:

  • Factual Verification: All admission regulations, fee structures, application dates, reservation criteria, and counselling steps are cross-referenced with officially released notifications from HSTES or HSBTE.
  • No Fabricated Dates: We never publish speculative or fake future update dates. When dates for an upcoming session are not yet officially released by HSTES, we clearly mark them as tentative or based on the previous academic cycle's schedule.
  • Regular Auditing: As state admission committees publish fresh information brochures, we review existing guides to reflect updated seat intakes, branch eligibility rules, and counselling schedules.
  • Community Error Reporting: If a student or teacher spots an inaccuracy, broken link, or outdated guideline, we review and correct it immediately upon notification.

Verified Official Government Portals

  • HSTES (Haryana State Technical Education Society): https://hstes.org.in — Official authority conducting Haryana LEET counselling.
