How reporting works after Haryana LEET seat allotment

The prospectus says candidates should first download the provisional allotment letter, pay the required semester fee, and then report to the allotted institute within the reporting window. The institute verifies uploaded documents, checks fee proof, and issues a system-generated provisional admission slip once everything is correct.

  • If any document is unclear or missing, the institute can raise a deficiency and ask for correction within the reporting dates.
  • If you fail to report within the prescribed period, the allotted seat can be cancelled automatically.
  • After successful reporting, keep the provisional admission slip as your proof of admission.

Main academic and identity documents

Qualifying examination proof

Bring the final certificate or proof of the diploma / qualifying examination used for admission.

Matriculation / high school certificate

Required for date of birth verification and identity consistency.

Character certificate

Regular candidates should use the prescribed format; private candidates need a certificate signed by a First Class Magistrate.

Aadhaar and fee proof

Carry Aadhaar number proof and proof of admission or tuition fee payment.

TFW, BC and income-related documents

For candidates using income-linked benefits, the prospectus keeps repeating one major rule: the annual family income should be correctly reflected and verifiable.

  • TFW: annual parental or guardian income from all sources should not exceed Rs 8.00 lakh.
  • BC category: annual parental income and creamy-layer conditions must match the applicable rule set.
  • EWS: original income and asset certificate is compulsory.
  • Where the prospectus mentions PPP verification, make sure the income/category details are already updated in the PPP system before final reporting.

Medical fitness, anti-ragging and final joining papers

Medical fitness certificate

Carry the prescribed medical fitness certificate for admission to B.E. / B.Tech. courses.

Anti-ragging undertaking by student

Student undertaking is compulsory at the time of joining.

Anti-ragging undertaking by parent / guardian

Parent or guardian undertaking must also be submitted.

Photographs

Keep passport-size colour photographs consistent with the images used in the application portal.

Physical joining notes from the prospectus

  • Bring all original documents and three sets of self-attested photocopies.
  • The institute should verify originals and then return them back to the candidate.
  • Under no circumstances should the institute retain the original certificates.
  • The institute and affiliating university may separately authenticate the admission documents after reporting.
If you are planning to upgrade in a later counselling round, read the counselling process page carefully. The prospectus notes that a newly allotted seat can automatically cancel the earlier seat once you participate in a subsequent round.

Frequently asked questions

How many document copies should I take to the institute?
Carry all originals and three self-attested photocopy sets. The prospectus says the photocopy sets are retained for registration and university authentication work.
Is medical fitness compulsory for Haryana LEET joining?
Yes, the reporting list includes the prescribed medical fitness certificate for B.E. / B.Tech. admissions.
Can the college keep my original marksheets?
No. The prospectus explicitly says the original certificates must be returned after verification and should not be retained by the institute.
