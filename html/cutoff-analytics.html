🔑 Counselling Category Abbreviations

During HSTES choice filling, you will notice different reservation seat category abbreviations. Here is what they represent:

  • AIC (All India Category): Open to all students nationwide (15% of total state seats). Highly competitive.
  • HOGC (Haryana Open General Category): Reserved for candidates belonging to Haryana state general category.
  • SC / DSC: Scheduled Castes / Deprived Scheduled Castes of Haryana.
  • BC-A / BC-B: Backward Classes block A and block B of Haryana state.
  • EWS (Economically Weaker Section): 10% separate reservation sub-category within General seats.
