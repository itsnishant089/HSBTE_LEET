Basic Eligibility – B.Tech (Lateral Entry)

  • Qualification: 3-year diploma in Engineering / Technology in a relevant branch from a recognized board (such as HSBTE or equivalent).
  • Minimum Percentage: Minimum aggregate percentage as prescribed by HSTES for the year (commonly around 45–50% for General and relaxation for reserved categories – check brochure for exact numbers).
  • Branch Relevance: Your diploma branch should be suitable for the B.Tech branch you are applying to (for example, Diploma in Mechanical for B.Tech Mechanical).
  • Backlogs: Candidates should satisfy conditions regarding backlogs / reappear subjects as mentioned by HSTES.

Eligibility – B.Pharmacy (Lateral Entry)

  • Qualification: Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm) from a recognized institute.
  • Minimum Percentage: Minimum aggregate marks in D.Pharm as per HSTES norms.
  • Registration: Additional conditions (if any) mentioned by Pharmacy Council / University should be checked in the brochure.

Possible B.Sc. Eligibility (If Notified)

In some lateral entry schemes, B.Sc. graduates with Mathematics may also be allowed for B.Tech LEET. This depends on the year’s rules.

  • B.Sc. degree with required subject combination (e.g. Mathematics) from a recognized university.
  • Minimum percentage and subject requirements as specified by HSTES.

Check the 2026 brochure to confirm whether B.Sc. candidates are included and under what conditions.

Domicile & Reservation Conditions

  • Haryana LEET has category-wise reservation rules (Haryana resident, SC, BC, EWS, etc.) as per state government policy.
  • Some seats may be open to All India category – check exact details in the brochure.
  • Candidates must have the required domicile certificates and category certificates at the time of counselling.

Important Points Before Applying

  • Read the HSTES official notification completely for Haryana LEET 2026.
  • Check eligibility separately for each college/university if they have additional conditions.
  • Ensure your diploma branch matches the B.Tech / B.Pharmacy course you want.
  • Keep documents ready – mark sheets, diploma certificate, category & domicile proofs etc.
