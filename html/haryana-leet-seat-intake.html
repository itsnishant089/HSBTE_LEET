Important intake rules from the prospectus

  • Appendix F itself says the displayed branch-wise intake is subject to confirmation and the final sanctioned intake is incorporated at the time of online counselling.
  • The prospectus notes that, as per AICTE, seats in B.Tech lateral entry may be taken up to a maximum of 10 percent of sanctioned intake.
  • Kashmiri Migrant, TFW and EWS seats are described as supernumerary, which means they are over and above the normal intake where applicable.
  • Vacant first-year seats and internal branch sliding can still affect the exact lateral-entry display at counselling time.
Best practice: use this page for planning, then confirm the final live seat display on techadmissionshry.gov.in before locking choices.

Branch-wise availability you should notice before counselling

CSE and allied branches

Computer Science and allied options dominate the intake list in 2026. Major government examples include YMCA 12, GJU 12, KUK 12, MDU 12 and DCRUST 12. Allied variants include AI and ML, Data Science, Cyber Security, IoT, Robotics and Electrical and Computer.

Mechanical and manufacturing branches

Mechanical stays available across many government institutes: YMCA 12, GJU 6, CRS SIET 6, Nilokheri 6, MDU 12, Panniwala 6 and DCRUST 6. SVSU also shows Mechanical and Smart Manufacturing 3.

Electrical and ECE branches

Electrical and ECE continue to appear regularly: YMCA 6, Gurugram University Electrical 3, GJU Electrical 6, Nilokheri Electrical 6, KUK ECE 12, MDU ECE 6, DCRUST ECE 12.

Civil branch

Civil is available in many government institutes with common intake around 6 seats: YMCA, GJU, CRS SIET, Nilokheri, MDU, Panniwala and DCRUST all show Civil intake.

Food, printing, packaging and plastic

Specialised options remain limited but important: GJU lists Food Technology 3, Packaging 3 and Printing Technology 6; Panniwala lists Food Technology 3; CIPET lists Plastic Technology 6.

Agriculture, biotech and biomedical

Agriculture appears at CCS HAU 4.1 and DCRUST 3. Biotechnology appears at KUK 6, MDU 6 and DCRUST 3. Biomedical appears at GJU 3 and DCRUST 3.

Hindi-medium engineering seats

The 2026 list includes a few Hindi-medium branch options in government universities, such as YMCA Computer and Mechanical, GJU CSE / ECE / IT / Mechanical, and DCRUST Electrical and Mechanical.

Women-focused and unique branches

BPSMV offers Fashion Technology 3 and IT 3 in addition to CSE and ECE. State Institute Panchkula lists Robotics and Automation 6, which is useful for students targeting automation-heavy branches.

How to use intake data well during choice filling

  • Do not look at intake alone. Pair it with previous cutoff trends, fees, location and branch priority.
  • Branches with more seats can still close early if demand is very high, especially CSE and AI-aligned branches.
  • Keep a mix of dream, realistic and safe choices.
  • Recheck the final counselling portal because the prospectus clearly says the live sanctioned intake may change before choice locking.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Appendix F intake final for Haryana LEET 2026?
No. Appendix F itself says the displayed intake is subject to confirmation, and the final sanctioned intake is shown during online counselling.
Which branches look strongest in government colleges for 2026?
Computer Science and allied branches remain the strongest in sheer availability, followed by Mechanical, Civil, Electrical and ECE. Government universities also show specialised branches like AI and ML, Data Science, Food Technology, Packaging, Printing and Robotics.
Are KM, TFW and EWS counted inside normal branch intake?
No. The prospectus treats them as supernumerary where applicable, which means they are separate from the normal sanctioned lateral-entry count.
