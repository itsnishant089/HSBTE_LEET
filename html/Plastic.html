About Plastic Technology

Plastic Technology focuses on polymer materials, plastic product manufacturing, and processing techniques used in modern industries. The diploma trains students in production, molding, and testing of plastic components.

Students learn polymer science, plastic processing machines, mold design basics, and quality control methods used in packaging, automotive, electronics, and consumer product industries.

What Students Learn

  • Polymer and plastic material science
  • Plastic processing methods
  • Injection molding technology
  • Blow molding and extrusion processes
  • Plastic product design basics
  • Mold and die fundamentals
  • Plastic testing and quality control
  • Plastic machinery operation
  • Recycling and sustainability basics
  • Industrial safety in polymer plants

Career Opportunities

  • Plastic Production Technician
  • Molding Machine Operator
  • Plastic Product Manufacturing Technician
  • Quality Control Technician (Polymer)
  • Plastic Processing Technician
  • Packaging Industry Technician
  • Polymer Plant Technician
  • Plastic Product Supervisor

Why Choose Plastic Technology

Plastic materials are used in almost every industry including automotive, packaging, electronics, medical, and construction. Skilled plastic technology diploma holders are in steady demand for manufacturing and production operations.

Real-World Applications

  • Injection molded product manufacturing
  • Plastic packaging production
  • Automotive plastic components
  • Household plastic product manufacturing
  • Plastic pipe and sheet extrusion
  • Polymer recycling processes
  • Medical plastic device production
  • Industrial plastic parts fabrication

Higher Education After This Diploma

After Plastic Technology diploma, students can pursue B.Tech in Polymer Engineering, Plastic Technology, Chemical Engineering, or Manufacturing through LEET and advance into design, production, or polymer research careers.
