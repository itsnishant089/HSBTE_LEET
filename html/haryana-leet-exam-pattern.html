Haryana LEET 2026 Exam Pattern

Know the marks distribution, sections and question paper format before attempting Haryana LEET 2026 PYQ and sample papers.

Official Pattern vs This Guide

The official Haryana LEET 2026 exam pattern is released only by HSTES. The structure given below is based on the typical pattern followed in recent years to help you plan your preparation. Always read the latest information brochure for final details.

Question Paper Structure (B.Tech LEET)

  • Question Type: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
  • Total Questions: usually around 90–100 MCQs
  • Total Marks: typically 90–100 marks (1 mark per question)
  • Exam Duration: about 2 hours (120 minutes)
  • Marking Scheme: +1 for correct answer, normally no negative marking (check latest brochure)
  • Mode of Exam: as notified by HSTES – refer to official notice

Indicative Section-wise Distribution

  • Mathematics – significant weightage (core diploma/11–12 topics)
  • Physics – conceptual + numerical questions
  • Basic Engineering / Computer Fundamentals – basic circuits, programming, logical reasoning
  • Branch-specific diploma subjects – core subjects of your stream

Exact number of questions in each section may vary year to year. Use PYQ and sample papers to understand the real trend.

Question Paper Structure (B.Pharmacy LEET)

  • Question Type: Objective MCQs
  • Total Questions & Marks: as per latest HSTES notification

Questions are usually distributed among:

  • Pharmaceutics
  • Pharmaceutical Chemistry
  • Pharmacology
  • Pharmacognosy
  • Pharmaceutical Analysis & Human Anatomy & Physiology

How to Use Exam Pattern for Preparation

  • Simulate real exam conditions when solving Haryana LEET sample papers or LEET PYQ.
  • Allocate time per section based on expected weightage – don’t spend too long on any single question.
  • Focus more on high-weightage areas such as Mathematics and branch-specific subjects.
  • Review your performance after every mock to adjust speed and accuracy.
