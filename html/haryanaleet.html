My Experience

I appeared for the Haryana LEET 2025 after completing my Diploma in Computer Engineering. Although the number of candidates was less, the question paper was very tough. I scored 40 out of 90 marks and secured Rank 50 among nearly 1,900 students. Currently, I am pursuing B.Tech in Computer Science from DCRUST. I also had the option to join YMCA through open counselling, but I chose DCRUST for better academic exposure and learning opportunities.

There are no specific books available that I found useful or worth following, so I did not waste time or money on them. Instead, I prepared by studying through ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and YouTube, which helped me understand concepts clearly and focus only on what was important for the exam.
