Haryana LEET Colleges Overview

Understand the types of B.Tech & B.Pharmacy colleges that offer admission through Haryana LEET lateral entry.

Last updated: 23 February 2026 • Always refer to official HSTES seat matrix and college list for the final status.

Where to Get Official College List

The exact and updated list of participating institutes and seats is published every year by HSTES in:

  • The Haryana LEET Information Brochure
  • Seat matrix / institute list on the HSTES website

Always check the latest documents for final college names, seat numbers and any changes in affiliation.

Types of Colleges Participating in Haryana LEET

  • State Government Universities / Institutes – e.g. technical universities and government engineering colleges.
  • Government Aided Institutions – institutes that receive government support but may have partially self-financed programs.
  • Private Self-Financed Colleges / Universities – many engineering and pharmacy colleges admit students through Haryana LEET ranks.

Different colleges may have different fee structures, facilities and placement records – analyse carefully before choosing.

How to Shortlist Colleges for Haryana LEET 2026

  • Check previous year cutoff to see which ranks were required for each college/branch.
  • Look at location preference – nearby city vs far-away district.
  • Review available branches (CSE, ECE, Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, etc.).
  • Check infrastructure, labs, faculty and placement records through official websites and student reviews.
  • Consider total fees, hostel availability and other facilities.

Engineering vs Pharmacy Colleges

  • Engineering Colleges (B.Tech LEET): Focus on branches like CSE, IT, ECE, EE, ME, CE, etc. Many polytechnic diploma students target these.
  • Pharmacy Colleges (B.Pharmacy LEET): Focus on B.Pharmacy lateral entry after D.Pharm, with different lab and approval requirements.

Check whether the institute is approved by the relevant bodies (AICTE/PCI/University) in the latest brochure.

Using Colleges Info During Counselling

  • Make a priority list of colleges before Haryana LEET 2026 counselling begins.
  • Include a mix of top, medium and safe colleges to improve chances of allotment.
  • Update your list after each counselling round based on cutoff and vacant seats.
