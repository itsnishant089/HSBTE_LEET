About Library & Information Science

Library & Information Science focuses on organizing, managing, and providing access to information resources in libraries, educational institutions, and digital systems. The HSBTE diploma prepares students for modern library management and information services.

Students learn cataloguing, classification, digital libraries, information retrieval, and library automation - enabling them to manage knowledge resources efficiently in academic, public, and organizational environments.

What Students Learn

  • Library classification systems
  • Cataloguing and indexing methods
  • Library management techniques
  • Digital library systems
  • Information organization
  • Documentation services
  • Library automation software
  • Reference services
  • Information retrieval systems
  • Library administration

Career Opportunities

  • Library Assistant
  • School / College Librarian Assistant
  • Documentation Assistant
  • Information Assistant
  • Digital Library Operator
  • Archive Assistant
  • Library Technician
  • Knowledge Resource Assistant

Why Choose Library Science

Libraries are evolving into digital knowledge centers. Skilled professionals are needed to organize information, manage digital resources, and support learning systems in schools, colleges, government institutions, and corporate organizations.

Real-World Applications

  • Managing school and college libraries
  • Organizing digital knowledge databases
  • Library automation and catalog systems
  • Information centers and archives
  • Documentation in offices and institutions
  • Digital repository management
  • Educational resource centers
  • Research information support

Higher Education After This Diploma

After Library & Information Science diploma, students can pursue Bachelor in Library Science (B.Lib.Sc), Information Science, or related degrees to become professional librarians or information managers.
