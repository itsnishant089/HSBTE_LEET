What is Haryana LEET? – Complete Overview

Haryana LEET (Lateral Entry Engineering Test) is conducted by the Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES) as an Online Computer Based Test (OCET). It provides diploma holders and B.Sc. graduates direct lateral entry admission into the 2nd year (3rd semester) of B.Tech and B.Pharmacy degree programmes in government and private institutions across Haryana.

Full FormLateral Entry Engineering Test
Conducting AuthorityHSTES (Haryana State Technical Education Society)
Exam TypeState Level Lateral Entry Entrance Test (OCET)
Exam ModeOnline – Computer Based Test (OCET) at designated centres
Question Type90 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) – English medium
Total Marks90 Marks (1 mark per question)
Duration90 Minutes
Negative Marking❌ No negative marking
Admissions Into2nd Year (3rd Semester) B.Tech / B.Pharmacy
Official Websitehstes.org.in / techeduhry.gov.in

⚖️ What is "HSBTE LEET" and Who Actually Conducts It (HSBTE vs HSTES)?

The phrase "HSBTE LEET" is widely searched by polytechnic students across Haryana, but there is an essential administrative distinction that every candidate must understand:

1. HSBTE (The Polytechnic Board)

Administers the 3-Year Polytechnic Diploma curriculum, sets semester exams, manages polytechnic results, and issues diploma certificates for all government and private polytechnics in Haryana.

Official: hsbte.org.in →

2. HSTES (The Admission Society)

The Haryana State Technical Education Society releases the official LEET brochure, conducts the entrance examination (OCET) / merit ranking, and manages centralized counselling on techadmissionshry.gov.in.

Official: techadmissionshry.gov.in →

Eligibility Criteria

🔧 BTech LEET Eligibility

  • ✓
    3-Year Diploma in Engineering / Technology from a recognized board (HSBTE or equivalent). Minimum 45% aggregate marks (40% for reserved category SC/BC/EWS candidates).
  • ✓
    B.Sc. Graduates with Mathematics in Class 12 and minimum 45% aggregate (40% reserved) are also eligible as per HSTES lateral entry norms.
  • ✓
    Branch Flexibility: Any engineering diploma branch holder can choose ANY B.Tech branch during counselling based on merit rank.
  • ✓
    Haryana Domicile required for state reservation quota seats (85% state quota).

💊 B.Pharmacy LEET Eligibility

  • ✓
    D.Pharmacy (Diploma in Pharmacy) from a PCI-approved institution with minimum 45% marks (40% for reserved category candidates).
  • ✓
    PCI Registration: Candidate must have passed from an institution recognized by the Pharmacy Council of India.
  • ✓
    Haryana Domicile required for state category seats.

Registration & Required Documents

Online registration for Haryana LEET is conducted via the official HSTES portal (hstes.org.in). Candidates must complete registration, upload required certificates, and pay the fee before the deadline.

  • ✓
    Registration Steps: Create an applicant account on hstes.org.in, fill personal and diploma academic marks, select preferred OCET test center, and pay application fee online.
  • ✓
    Required Documents: Keep digital copies ready: Class 10th certificate (DOB proof), diploma marksheet (all semesters / final year), Haryana domicile certificate (if applicable), caste/category certificate (SC/BC/EWS), and valid photo identity proof.
🪪OCET Exam Documents Checklist → 📂Admission Document Verification List →

Mandatory for Haryana Domicile: Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP ID)

Under Haryana Government norms, entering a valid 8-digit Parivar Pehchan Patra (Family ID) is mandatory on techadmissionshry.gov.in to claim the 85% Haryana State Quota, caste reservations (SC / Deprived SC / BCA / BCB), Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW), or EWS benefits. Non-Haryana applicants (15% All India Category) can apply without a PPP ID.

Preparation Strategy

Analyse the Syllabus & Official Exam Pattern

Download the HSTES official syllabus PDF. Note the 4-section structure (A: Basic Sciences 25M, B: Electronics 25M, C: Mechanical 20M, D: Other Streams 20M). Focus on high-weightage topics.

Solve PYQ in OCET Format

Set a 90-minute timer and attempt 90 questions. Since it is a computer-based test (OCET), practice on a screen. No negative marking — always attempt all questions.

Use Free Online Resources

ChatGPT, Google Gemini and YouTube are highly effective. Search topic-wise explanations instead of buying expensive books (rarely available for LEET).

Revise Diploma Textbooks

Go through 5th/6th semester diploma textbooks for branch subjects. HSBTE diploma notes directly align with the LEET syllabus.

Focus on Math & Electronics (50 Marks Total)

Sections A+B together carry 50 out of 90 marks. Math (8Q) and Electronics (25Q) are the highest-yield areas. Practise these daily.

Attempt All 90 Questions

With zero negative marking, never leave any question blank. Make an educated guess if unsure — this alone can add 5–10 extra marks.

Counselling & Choice Filling

After the declaration of Haryana LEET results and inter-se-merit lists, eligible candidates participate in online centralized counselling managed by HSTES.

  1. Result & Merit List: HSTES publishes the LEET scorecard and state merit list on hstes.org.in. Verify your overall and category rank.
  2. Counselling Registration & Fee: Register on the HSTES counselling portal and submit the mandatory non-refundable counselling fee online.
  3. Document Verification: Keep digital copies ready for verification: diploma marksheets, Haryana domicile certificate, caste/category certificate, and Class 10th certificate.
  4. Choice Filling & Locking: Fill maximum preferred college and branch combinations in order of priority. Always lock choices before the window closes; unlocked choices automatically freeze based on the last saved order.
  5. Seat Allotment (Round 1 & Round 2): Check allotment status online. If allotted a seat, pay the seat confirmation fee or opt for seat upgradation in the next round.
  6. Physical Reporting: Report to the allotted institute with original certificates and course fees within the designated reporting window.

Use the OCET checklist, admission document checklist, and seat intake breakdown for informed choice filling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there negative marking in Haryana LEET 2027?▼
No — there is no negative marking. Each correct answer = +1 mark. Wrong or unattempted = 0 marks. Always attempt all 90 questions. Confirm from official 2027 HSTES brochure.
What is the exam mode of Haryana LEET 2027?▼
Haryana LEET is an Online Computer Based Test (OCET). Candidates go to designated test centres and answer 90 MCQs on a computer in 90 minutes. The medium is English only.
What is the section-wise marks distribution for BTech LEET?▼
Section A (Basic Sciences): 25 marks | Section B (Electronics Stream): 25 marks | Section C (Mechanical Stream): 20 marks | Section D (Other Engineering Streams): 20 marks. Total: 90 marks.
Can I choose any B.Tech branch regardless of my diploma branch?▼
Yes. According to the official HSTES brochure, a candidate with any engineering diploma can choose admission in any B.Tech branch during counselling based on rank and seat availability. For example, a Diploma in Mechanical holder can take admission in Computer Engineering.
How many months of preparation is enough for LEET?▼
2–3 months of focused study is sufficient. Since it is an online computer-based test (OCET), practice on a computer. Solve 3–5 PYQs in timed sessions (90 min). Since there is no negative marking, always attempt all 90 questions.
What is the Haryana LEET 2027 cutoff like?▼
Cutoff varies by college, branch and category. In 2027, scoring 40/90 was enough for Rank 50 among ~1,900 candidates. Top colleges (DCRUST, YMCA) close at lower rank numbers. Check previous year cutoff data for planning.
