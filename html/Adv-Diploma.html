About Advanced Diploma in Tool & Die

Advanced Diploma in Tool & Die is a specialized mechanical engineering programme under HSBTE Haryana, focused on the design, manufacturing, and maintenance of precision tools, dies, molds, jigs, and fixtures used in large-scale industrial production. This critical field powers mass manufacturing across automotive, aerospace, electronics, and consumer goods sectors, making tool & die professionals indispensable in modern industry.

The programme combines theoretical foundations in engineering drawing, material science, and manufacturing processes with extensive hands-on training on CNC machines, EDM, and precision measuring instruments — preparing graduates for immediate industry roles.

What Students Learn

  • Tool and die design principles
  • CAD/CAM and CNC machining
  • Mold and press tool manufacturing
  • Metrology and precision measurement
  • Material science for tooling applications
  • Production and manufacturing processes
  • EDM (Electrical Discharge Machining)
  • Heat treatment of tool steels
  • Jig and fixture design
  • Quality assurance and GD&T

Career Opportunities

  • Tool & Die Designer
  • CNC Programmer / Operator
  • Mold Design Engineer
  • Production Engineer
  • Quality Control Engineer
  • Manufacturing Technician
  • Maintenance Engineer (Toolroom)
  • CAD/CAM Specialist

Why Choose Tool & Die Engineering

Tool & Die professionals are among the most valued engineers in the manufacturing industry. Every mass-produced product — from a car door panel to a smartphone casing — starts with a precision tool or die. Skilled professionals enjoy strong demand across India and internationally, competitive salaries, and clear pathways to senior engineering and managerial roles. With Industry 4.0 driving demand for precision automation, the field is evolving rapidly with excellent long-term growth prospects.

Real-World Applications

  • Automobile body and engine component molds
  • Plastic injection molds for consumer products
  • Sheet-metal press tools
  • Electronic device casing molds
  • Aerospace precision components
  • Pharmaceutical packaging dies
  • White goods (appliance) component tooling
  • Defence and heavy engineering parts

Higher Education After This Diploma

After completing the Advanced Diploma in Tool & Die from HSBTE, students are eligible to apply for Haryana LEET (Lateral Entry Entrance Test) for direct admission to the 2nd year of B.Tech Mechanical or Manufacturing Engineering programmes at Haryana state universities — a fast-track route to a full engineering degree without repeating common subjects.
