What this OCET checklist covers

The B.Tech lateral entry prospectus separates the process into two parts: the documents you must upload while registering on techadmissionshry.gov.in and the documents you must carry to the exam centre on test day. Students often mix these two steps, so this page keeps them separate.

Registration uploads Test-day ID proof Print application + admit card PPP and category checks
Prospectus note: Haryana candidates register using PPP ID or Aadhaar and key personal details such as name, date of birth, parent name, category and income are fetched from the PPP database. Those fetched fields are treated as non-editable during registration.

Documents to upload during Haryana LEET OCET registration

Keep these files ready before starting the B.Tech LEET application form:

Passport photo

Recent coloured photograph in JPG or JPEG format with a maximum size of 30 KB.

Specimen signature

Signature in JPG or JPEG format with a maximum size of 20 KB.

10th DMC / matric proof

PDF copy used for date of birth verification. The prospectus specifically asks for matric proof during application.

Qualifying examination DMC

Upload the detailed marks certificate of the qualifying examination if available at the time of application.

Category / special category PDFs

Relevant PDFs for documents not fetched from PPP, especially EWS, KM, ESM, FF and other special-category claims.

Identity details for sign-up

Use PPP ID or Aadhaar for Haryana candidates. Candidates from other states register using Aadhaar.

Also keep these registration details ready

  • Your personal mobile number and email ID for advisories and notices.
  • Three preferred OCET centre choices while filling the form.
  • Course selection as B.E./B.Tech. (Lateral Entry) and special category selection, if applicable.
  • Online registration fee: Rs. 1000 for General category and Rs. 700 for reserved categories.
The prospectus clearly states that applications without successful online fee payment will not be considered for OCET-2026, and duplicate applications are not allowed.

Documents to carry to the OCET test centre

After downloading the admit card, the prospectus asks candidates to reach the allotted centre with the following papers:

  • Two printed copies of the admit card with photograph attested by a gazetted officer.
  • One valid photo identity proof for invigilator verification.
  • Copy or attested copy of matriculation certificate for date of birth verification before entry.
  • PPP copy if the candidate belongs to Haryana State.
  • Printed application form and fee confirmation for personal record and mismatch checks.

Important admit card rules

  • The admit card must be downloaded by the candidate from the portal; HSTES will not post it separately.
  • One admit card copy must be signed by the invigilator and retained by the candidate.
  • Do not write or alter any entry on the admit card.
  • Only the allotted shift and allotted centre mentioned on the admit card are valid.

Step-by-step OCET registration flow

Stage What the prospectus says
Step I Sign up for B.Tech lateral entry, create login credentials, and note the unique registration number.
Step II Fill other relevant details such as category, sub-category and PPP information.
Step III Upload photograph and signature in the prescribed size and format.
Step IV Fill qualifying examination details and keep the marks data accurate.
Step V Confirm the filled registration details before locking the form.
Step VI Upload PDF documents required to verify registration details.
Step VII Deposit registration fee online using debit card, credit card, net banking or UPI.

Exam day reporting rules and banned items

  • Reach the allotted OCET centre at least 30 minutes before the start of the test.
  • The online test duration is 90 minutes, and the prospectus also mentions 15 minutes for filling required data during the test process.
  • You may ask the invigilator for help if you are unfamiliar with the system allotted to you.
  • Books, papers, slide rules, log tables, calculators, cellular phones, pagers, smart watches and digital diaries are not allowed.
  • Use of unfair means or wrong information can lead to cancellation of candidature.

Frequently asked questions

Is the PPP copy required at the OCET centre?
Yes, the prospectus specifically says candidates belonging to Haryana State are required to bring the copy of PPP ID along with them for verification.
Can I edit my form after PPP details are fetched?
Only the details not fetched and verified from the PPP database can be edited. The PPP-fetched fields are treated as non-editable.
What happens if I forget to carry the admit card?
The prospectus says no candidate shall be allowed to appear in the entrance test without the admit card. Carry both printed copies and a valid photo ID.
