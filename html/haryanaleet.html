Exam Overview – Haryana LEET 2026

Haryana LEET (Lateral Entry Engineering Test) is conducted by HSTES as an Online Computer Based Test (OCET) for diploma holders seeking direct admission to the 2nd year of B.Tech / B.Pharmacy in Haryana colleges.

Full FormLateral Entry Engineering Test
Conducting AuthorityHSTES (Haryana State Technical Education Society)
Exam TypeState Level Entrance Test (OCET)
Exam ModeOnline – Computer Based Test (OCET) at test centres
Question Type90 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) – English only
Total Marks90 Marks (1 mark per question)
Duration90 Minutes
Negative Marking❌ No negative marking
Admissions Into2nd Year (3rd Semester) B.Tech / B.Pharmacy
Official Websitehstes.org.in / techeduhry.gov.in

Eligibility Criteria

🔧 BTech LEET Eligibility

  • ✓
    3-Year Diploma in Engineering / Technology from a recognized board (HSBTE or equivalent). Minimum 45% aggregate (relaxation for SC/BC/EWS).
  • ✓
    B.Sc. Graduates with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics may also be eligible for certain B.Tech branches as per HSTES norms.
  • ✓
    Branch Flexibility: Any engineering diploma branch can apply for ANY B.Tech branch during counselling.
  • ✓
    Haryana Domicile required for state quota seats.

💊 B.Pharmacy LEET Eligibility

  • ✓
    D.Pharmacy (Diploma in Pharmacy) from a recognized institution approved by PCI. Minimum marks as per HSTES norms.
  • ✓
    PCI Registration: Should be registered or eligible to register as a Pharmacist.
  • ✓
    Haryana Domicile required for state category seats.

Preparation Strategy

Analyse the Syllabus & Official Exam Pattern

Download the HSTES official syllabus PDF. Note the 4-section structure (A: Basic Sciences 25M, B: Electronics 25M, C: Mechanical 20M, D: Other Streams 20M). Focus on high-weightage topics.

Solve PYQ in OCET Format

Set a 90-minute timer and attempt 90 questions. Since it is a computer-based test (OCET), practice on a screen. No negative marking — always attempt all questions.

Use Free Online Resources

ChatGPT, Google Gemini and YouTube are highly effective. Search topic-wise explanations instead of buying expensive books (rarely available for LEET).

Revise Diploma Textbooks

Go through 5th/6th semester diploma textbooks for branch subjects. HSBTE diploma notes directly align with the LEET syllabus.

Focus on Math & Electronics (50 Marks Total)

Sections A+B together carry 50 out of 90 marks. Math (8Q) and Electronics (25Q) are the highest-yield areas. Practise these daily.

Attempt All 90 Questions

With zero negative marking, never leave any question blank. Make an educated guess if unsure — this alone can add 5–10 extra marks.

Counselling Process

After the Haryana LEET 2026 result, eligible candidates register for online counselling through the HSTES portal.

  1. Result & Merit List: HSTES publishes LEET 2026 result and state merit list on hstes.org.in. Download your scorecard.
  2. Counselling Registration: Register on HSTES counselling portal. Pay counselling fee online.
  3. Document Verification: Upload: Diploma certificates, marksheets, domicile certificate, category certificate, class 10th certificate.
  4. Choice Filling & Locking: Fill college + branch preferences in order. Lock before deadline — changes not allowed after locking.
  5. Seat Allotment Rounds: Multiple rounds (typically 2–3). Pay seat acceptance fee if allotted. Wait for upgradation in next round if desired.
  6. Report to College: Visit allotted college for physical document verification and fee payment within the reporting date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there negative marking in Haryana LEET 2026?▼
No — there is no negative marking. Each correct answer = +1 mark. Wrong or unattempted = 0 marks. Always attempt all 90 questions. Confirm from official 2026 HSTES brochure.
What is the exam mode of Haryana LEET 2026?▼
Haryana LEET is an Online Computer Based Test (OCET). Candidates go to designated test centres and answer 90 MCQs on a computer in 90 minutes. The medium is English only.
What is the section-wise marks distribution for BTech LEET?▼
Section A (Basic Sciences): 25 marks | Section B (Electronics Stream): 25 marks | Section C (Mechanical Stream): 20 marks | Section D (Other Engineering Streams): 20 marks. Total: 90 marks.
Can I choose any B.Tech branch regardless of my diploma branch?▼
Yes. According to the official HSTES brochure, a candidate with any engineering diploma can choose admission in any B.Tech branch during counselling based on rank and seat availability. For example, a Diploma in Mechanical holder can take admission in Computer Engineering.
How many months of preparation is enough for LEET?▼
2–3 months of focused study is sufficient. Since it is an online computer-based test (OCET), practice on a computer. Solve 3–5 PYQs in timed sessions (90 min). Since there is no negative marking, always attempt all 90 questions.
What is the Haryana LEET 2026 cutoff like?▼
Cutoff varies by college, branch and category. In 2025, scoring 40/90 was enough for Rank 50 among ~1,900 candidates. Top colleges (DCRUST, YMCA) close at lower rank numbers. Check previous year cutoff data for planning.
