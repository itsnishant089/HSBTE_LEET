B.Pharmacy LEET 2027-27 Overview

This schedule is tentative. Always verify the latest B.Pharmacy lateral entry notice on the HSTES portal before making payment or reporting to the institute.
  • The B.Pharmacy lateral entry course duration mentioned in the shared schedule is 3 years.
  • Registration and fee payment both run from 26 May 2027 to 9 July 2027.
  • The official helpdesk email is onlineadmissionhelp@gmail.com.
  • The toll-free helpline is 1800-137-3735 between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM.

Practical Planning Tips for B.Pharmacy Students

  • Keep D.Pharmacy marksheets, category certificates and ID proof ready before 26 May 2027.
  • Download the admit card as soon as it opens on 10 July 2027 instead of waiting until exam day.
  • After the merit result on 20 July 2027, shortlist realistic pharmacy institutes before round 1 opens.
  • Track both choice filling and reporting windows carefully because the rounds are close together.
