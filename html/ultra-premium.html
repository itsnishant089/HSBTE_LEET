LEET 2027 Exam Strategy Tips

1. Attempt Section A (Basic Sciences) first - predictable topics, high accuracy
2. Do Mathematics when mind is fresh - skip lengthy ones and return
3. No negative marking - attempt all 90 questions without hesitation
4. Keep 10 minutes at end for unattempted and review
5. Elimination technique - remove 2 wrong options first
6. Don't panic if paper is difficult - others face the same
7. Section D (Other streams) is often easier - check early
8. 90 questions in 90 minutes = 1 minute per question strategy
